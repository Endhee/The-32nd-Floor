Hello, Mbakara mmi, ❤️

I have tried to put my thoughts and words together to describe how I feel about today, and more importantly, how I feel about you. Words might not be enough, but here goes nothing...

Today is not just another day. It is the day you were born, and I don't want you to see it as ordinary. You're so special, and it's your special day. I want you to feel like the king that you are. Now, let's talk about YOU!!

We all know life has a funny way of introducing people into our lives, and our story is a funny one, too. Sometimes, we meet people without knowing the memories we will eventually create with them. That is our story.

Who would have thought that a random guy I admired on the streets would turn out to be my most favourite person? From our random talks, to the funny jokes, to the serious conversations, and the fights, and even the moments we just paused and simply enjoyed each other's company. I have come to love and appreciate every little experience.

You might not realise it, but there are things I so much like about you; your personality, your humour, your stubbornness and even those moments you get overly dramatic over nothing. 😂 I enjoy every bit of it.

Well, I won't pretend that I know it all or that I have always understood you or had the perfect words to describe you. But one thing I know is that meeting you has given me memories I don't want to erase.

There is a line in a song that says, "I bless the day I found you"... and yes, my love, I bless the day our paths crossed.

What is this one trying to say all along; YOU ARE GREAT and don't allow the present conditions or situations to stop you from dreaming and becoming LASHUPFX, a world-renowned multimedia company. I'm rooting for you, baby.

I pray that God blesses this new chapter of your life. May He open doors that no one can shut, connect you with people who will genuinely support your growth and grant you success that will make all the sacrifices and stress worthwhile.

May you never beg for opportunities, may your efforts speak for you, and may happiness never become a stranger to your life. Amen. 🙏🙏
